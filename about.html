About me?

I’m a Security Research Associate at Appknox, where I’ve been digging into app security for the past 2.5 years. Kicked things off as an intern back in late 2022, and it clicked so well I stuck around full-time.

My first six months were all about pentesting—mostly web and Android apps. I got comfy spotting those sneaky vulnerabilities, chaining exploits, and writing up solid reports. Then I switched gears to iOS, putting iOS front and center since that’s where a lot of the fun (and headaches) hides.

Heading into year two, I got into tweak development and reversing. Started simple: learning the ropes with jailbroken iOS devices, tweaking code, and building basic mods. At the same time, I got flexible with pentesting across Android, iOS, and web—no more silos, just whatever the project threw at me.

These days, I’m messing around with AI agents on my own time—trying to automate the boring parts of mobile security research. On the side, I’m trying to get into iOS vuln hunting—just started poking at IPSW files. Barely past the basics, but it’s pretty interesting.

Outside work, I’m always tinkering with AI agents and automation stuff. I also get lost in books on human evolution—stuff like how we got from caves to code—and exploring different faiths and philosophies. It keeps the big-picture thinking sharp.
